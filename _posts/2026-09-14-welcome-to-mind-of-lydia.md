Welcome to Mind of Lydia. I’m glad you’re here.

This is a place for honest stories about motherhood and family life. I want to make room for the moments that are joyful, the ones that feel hard, and the ordinary days that rarely make it into a polished picture. None of us has to have every answer before we can share what we are learning.

I’ll also write about the things that shape life around family: mental wellness, faith, creative work, and the places we discover. As this site grows, you’ll find Maryland home guidance and The Lydia Rating, my approach to food and product reviews.

My hope is simple: that you leave a story feeling seen, encouraged, or a little more curious about what comes next. Thank you for being part of the beginning.
