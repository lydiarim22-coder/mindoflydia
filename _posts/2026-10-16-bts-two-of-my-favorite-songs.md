I'm starting my Korean Culture music collection with BTS and two of my favorite songs from them: "Butterfly" and "Dimple."

If you're exploring their music, these are two songs I'd like to share.

## Butterfly

**Album:** *The Most Beautiful Moment in Life: Young Forever*  
**Album release:** May 2, 2016

"Butterfly" is one of my BTS favorites. You can find it on *Young Forever*, which also includes a Prologue Mix and an Alternative Mix of the song.

[Explore Young Forever on BTS's official website](https://bts.ibighit.com/eng/discography/detail/hwayangyeonhwa-young_forever.html) or [listen to the album on Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/1598661880).

## Dimple

**Album:** *Love Yourself: Her*  
**Album release:** September 18, 2017

"Dimple" is another favorite of mine. It is track four on *Love Yourself: Her*.

[Explore Love Yourself: Her on BTS's official website](https://bts.ibighit.com/eng/discography/detail/love_yourself-her.html) or [listen to Dimple on Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1596529068).
