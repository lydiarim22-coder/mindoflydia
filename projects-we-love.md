<h2>Fuselyn</h2>
<p>Fuselyn is a suite of free tools that supports students from choosing a college to staying on track once enrolled. College Intel estimates admissions odds using SAT or ACT scores, Admissions Services turns a shortlist into a Common App-ready plan, Overseas Intel helps students explore universities in the UK and Ireland, and Study Tracker helps them stay on pace during college.</p>
