<h2>Fuselyn</h2>
<p>From exploring colleges to keeping up with classes, Fuselyn brings together tools for each stage of a student's journey. Developed by <a href="https://katelandinc.com/">Kateland INC</a>, the suite helps students research their options, organize applications, explore universities abroad, and build a study routine once enrolled.</p>

<ul>
  <li><a href="https://collegeintel.fuselyn.com/">College Intel</a> helps students explore U.S. colleges using SAT or ACT scores, compare schools, and discover potential college and major matches.</li>
  <li><a href="https://admissions.fuselyn.com/">Admissions Services</a> turns a college shortlist into an application plan, with school-specific fit information, checklists, and a roadmap for the work ahead.</li>
  <li><a href="https://collegeintel.fuselyn.com/overseas">Overseas Intel</a>, now part of College Intel's UK &amp; Ireland section, helps students explore universities abroad, compare entry requirements, and find admissions guides.</li>
  <li><a href="https://plan.fuselyn.com/">Study Tracker</a> helps students organize coursework, plan study time, and review with flashcards. Manual tasks, flashcards, and study groups are free; AI planning features are available through a paid upgrade.</li>
</ul>
